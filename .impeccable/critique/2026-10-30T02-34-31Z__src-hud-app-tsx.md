## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This screen is clearly authored for this product, and the AP timeline proves it. A 0–300 AP track with a dashed next-turn ghost, amber hatching for the sweep window, red diamonds for pending effects and a break-free bar exists nowhere else. The Incoming panel's wording ("Jubal acts in 15: can still escape (+55)") is a rules instrument talking, not a game UI. The Forge Ledger palette is applied consistently.

It turns generic in two places:
- **The board overlays.** Tinted reach tiles, a line path and a corner label with a leader line read like any grid tactics game.
- **The 3D board.** The pawns, glossy block boss and perspective grid are competent but conventional. 3D adds drama, especially the red sweep slab, but carries less product voice than the timeline.

The biggest missed chance is provenance, the product's second principle. It's nearly invisible during play: one tiny line in the unit card, and nothing beside the Incoming numbers, the ghost's projected AP, or the result table.

## Overall Impression

The hard, unique parts are designed better than the basics. The best moment on this screen is when a sweep is telegraphed: red tiles, a "Sweeping Arm · 70 AP" tag, a red timeline diamond and green "can still escape" lines all agree. But the moment a playtester actually needs, "is this tile safe if I stand here?", has no plain verdict anywhere, and the click that decides it has no safety net. Biggest opportunity: turn the ghost from a marker into a verdict.

## What's Working

1. The timeline ghost (Timeline.tsx): hovering an action shows your projected next turn in place on the AP track; teaches "AP spent = delay" better than prose; tints red/amber on danger.
2. The Incoming panel's wording: per-unit verdicts from the same rules code as execution.
3. The Forge Ledger world, held with discipline: soot ground, bronze labels, serif names, sans numbers, mono log keep the semantic colors clear of the surrounding UI.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The ghost doesn't say "safe" or "caught"**
- Why it matters: escaping a telegraphed blow is the lesson the demo exists to teach. Hovering the only reachable sweep-zone tile on Nephilim put a plain white ghost at 50 AP inside the 40–140 hatch; safe vs caught requires comparing the pill to the hatch's right edge by eye.
- Fix: labeled "lands" end cap on the hatch; ghost Ready Green when it acts before landing (red/amber kept for caught/risk); verdict in words beside the ghost ("safe: you act at 50, it lands at 140"); show "no escape" without needing a hover.
- Suggested command: $impeccable clarify

**[P1] Committing into danger has no net, and failed actions don't explain themselves**
- Why it matters: playtesters will learn by dying to the mechanic the demo should have shown them. A sweep-zone click moves Jubal immediately; Attack mode doesn't draw range/pattern; controller.ts:316's toast doesn't name the failing condition.
- Fix: red pre-commit warning state when the hovered move is "no escape"; draw the weapon's reachable pattern on hover in Attack mode; name the failing condition (out of range, pattern doesn't reach, line of sight blocked); treat a same-turn take-back as a rules decision for Aaron.
- Suggested command: $impeccable harden

**[P1] Board overlays are weak in both views, and labels sit too far from what they describe**
- Why it matters: the whole product depends on reading overlays at a glance.
- What's wrong: blue reach at 22% alpha is pale on tan floors and almost invisible on Road Wolves in 3D; path is white (DESIGN.md: Path Blue); hover labels go to the top lane with leaders crossing ~600px of board (in 3D crossing boss and pawns); 2D board uses about half the canvas.
- Fix: raise reach opacity and add a 1px Path Blue edge; Path Blue path with end marker; labels within 1–2 tiles of their tile, lane only for overflow; scale 2D board to fill; in 3D, larger plates and glyphs and keep labels/leaders off tokens.
- Suggested command: $impeccable layout

**[P2] Provenance disappears during play**
- Why it matters: principle 2 applies where Aaron makes judgments. Provenance lives in the Rules drawer and one tiny unit-card line; Incoming numbers, ghost AP, damage previews and result table carry no source tier.
- Fix: compact provenance mark beside tunable numbers with badge and rationale on hover; rules-and-sources block in the result report.
- Suggested command: $impeccable shape

**[P2] Type scale and density overload the left panel and the timeline**
- Why it matters: cognitive-load checklist fails 4 of 8 (high).
- What's wrong: left panel stacks 8+ blocks; tick numbers and captions at 10px; 12 off-scale font sizes; unit card ready chips below the fold at 900px; board ~410px at 1180px wide.
- Fix: consolidate to DESIGN.md's scale (11, 12, 14, 17/18, 22); 11px minimum; left panel in three chunks (turn status / actions / unit); collapse unit-card stats behind a disclosure.
- Suggested command: $impeccable typeset, then $impeccable distill

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-time playtester):** no guide strip on Shrine and Nephilim; first view is an 8-block left panel and a 0–300 timeline with an undecodable amber hatch; "Wait → next in 25 AP" is the most prominent action; Z, S, r and W need hovering; first click can move them into the sweep zone with no warning.

**Alex (power user):** hotkeys exist but no keyboard cursor on the board and no full legend; every preview needs a hover and a glance to a far-corner label; no take-back or restart-from-this-turn.

**Sam (accessibility):** bronze labels at exactly 4.50:1 at 11px; 10px tick labels and badges; floating MISS/DODGE at 3.2:1 on road floor, saved only by outline; safe/risk/caught mostly color-carried; no board keyboard access; no prefers-reduced-motion handling (verified).

**Aaron (designer tuning rules):** no source tier beside any number he judges in play; no "which rule produced this number" hover; result modal makes "Retry (same seed)" primary while "Copy report" is secondary with no share link and toasts "paste it to Brendan" (Screens.tsx:196); failed attacks give no diagnostic.

## Minor Observations

- DESIGN.md is wrong about victory: it lists victory under Ready Green, but the UI (styles.css:170) uses Forge Gold. DESIGN.md needs the fix, not the UI.
- Granary guide strip background (#26302a) is a desaturated green-gray close to the reserved semantic hues; could be a Hearth Brown band with a gold step label.
- Enemy turns leave a large empty "…is acting" box in the left panel; could show the actor's card and what it just did.
- "Jb" is the only two-letter glyph and breaks the pill rhythm; pills collide at AP 0.
- The best copy on the screen, "Kill him before he tears free of the rubble", is hover-only on the break-free counter.
- "Sweep can target yourself." shows under Attack mode for weapons it doesn't apply to.
- 3D "Drag to orbit · wheel to zoom" hint is 11px faded text (detector: tiny).
- About 20 HUD tints were never made into tokens; worth an $impeccable extract pass later.
